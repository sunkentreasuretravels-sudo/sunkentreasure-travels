# WorldWebMaster

WorldWebMaster is the reusable subsystem for world geography, places, experiences, media, marine activity and contributor content.

## The six engines

### 1. World
Countries, territories, regions and geography.

### 2. Places
Destinations, hubs, ports, cities and future real-estate locations.

### 3. Experiences
Events, branded experiences, attractions and programs.

### 4. Media
Licensed destination media, video, galleries and traveler submissions.

### 5. Intelligence
Relationships between countries, places, events, experiences and media.

### 6. Marine
Cruise corridors, ports, verified ship/line activity and ocean-region interactions.

## Media principle
Never hard-code image paths into business logic. Media is data.

Resolution:
specific asset -> country/destination asset -> regional asset -> global fallback.

## Contributor principle
Visitors can submit photos/video. Automated screening can flag content. Human approval is required before publication.

## Domain principle
An experience can begin as a route inside a host website and later move to its own domain without rebuilding the experience.

## Brand principle
WorldWebMaster is infrastructure. It does not merge the identities of the businesses using it.
