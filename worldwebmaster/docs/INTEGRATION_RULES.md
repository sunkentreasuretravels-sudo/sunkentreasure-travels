# Integration Rules

## Rule 1 — Never replace a finished brand website
Integrate WorldWebMaster into the existing project.

## Rule 2 — Keep brand content outside the engine
The engine must not own a brand's homepage, brand copy, logo, colors,
business-specific navigation or proprietary content.

## Rule 3 — Use adapters
A brand project should have a thin adapter/configuration layer that maps:
- its destinations
- its events
- its experiences
- its media
- its navigation
into WorldWebMaster.

## Rule 4 — One engine, many brands
Improvements to WorldWebMaster should be reusable by every participating
brand.

## Rule 5 — Media is data
Never require manual image-path editing for ordinary media updates.

## Rule 6 — Approval before publication
Contributor photos/videos remain private/pending until approved.

## Rule 7 — Domain portability
Experiences can begin as internal routes and later move to independent domains.
